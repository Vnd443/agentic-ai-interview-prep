# Behavioral & FDE — Cheatsheet

> Just the topic list. Write your own one-line explanation next to each in your own words.
> (Full explanations are in `concepts.md`.)

## STAR ⭐
- Situation (short) → Task → Action (most of it, what YOU did) → Result —
- End every story on a number —

## Quantify
- %, time saved, cost cut, users, latency, error rate —
- "cut p95 4s → 900ms, tickets −30%" beats "it went well" —

## Flagship stories
- Prep 3–5 real stories, each mapped to many prompts —
- Capsule wardrobe: recombine a few for any question —

## FDE mindset ⭐
- Customer-facing, ships fast, owns ambiguity end-to-end —
- Discovery → build → deploy → support —
- Lead story: dropped in ambiguity → scoped → shipped fast → quantified —

## Stakeholder translation
- Vague business ask → scoped AI solution —
- Explain cost/latency/accuracy in their language —

## Speed vs. correctness
- Ship good-enough + iterate by default —
- Name the corners you won't cut (irreversible / safety / data) —

## Ownership
- Own the whole arc, not a slice —

## Questions to ask them (3–5)
- "What does success look like in first 90 days?" —
- "Customer velocity vs. platform quality — how do you balance?" —
- + role/company-specific —

## Numbers / facts worth quoting
- Aim for 3–5 flagship STAR stories —
- Every story ends with one metric —
