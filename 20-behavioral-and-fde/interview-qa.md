# Behavioral & FDE — Interview Q&A

> Starter set (not from the guide PDF). Answer these in **STAR** format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — and *quantify* results.
> FDE = Forward Deployed Engineer: customer-facing, ships fast, owns ambiguity end-to-end.

---

## How to use this file
Draft one STAR story per prompt below, reusing your real IBM / GenAI work. Aim for **3–5 flagship stories** you can flex to many questions. End every story with a **number** (%, time saved, cost cut, users, latency).

---

## Behavioral prompts (draft a STAR answer for each)

1. **Ship under ambiguity** — "Tell me about a time requirements were unclear and you had to make progress anyway."
2. **Ownership** — "A project was failing / off track. What did you do?"
3. **Working with a customer/stakeholder** — "Describe translating a vague business ask into a working AI solution." *(core FDE signal)*
4. **Conflict / disagreement** — "You disagreed with a teammate or lead on an approach."
5. **Failure / mistake** — "Something you shipped broke. What happened and what did you learn?"
6. **Learning fast** — "You had to pick up a new tech/domain quickly." *(agentic tools, MCP, a new AWS service)*
7. **Impact under constraints** — "Delivered value with limited time/budget/data." *(cost optimization story fits here)*
8. **Influence without authority** — "Got others to adopt your idea/tool."

---

## FDE-specific angles

- **Customer empathy:** how you handled a frustrated customer / shifting requirements on-site.
- **Speed vs. correctness:** when you shipped a "good enough" solution fast and iterated — and when you *didn't* cut the corner.
- **End-to-end ownership:** a time you owned discovery → build → deploy → support.
- **Bridging technical ↔ business:** explaining a trade-off (cost, latency, accuracy) to a non-technical stakeholder.

**🔑 Power move:** For an FDE role, lead with a story where you were **dropped into ambiguity at a customer, scoped it, shipped fast, and quantified the impact** — that's the whole job in one story.
